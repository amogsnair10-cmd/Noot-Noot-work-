## Week 5: Short i, o, u words

### Lesson 21: Short i (pig, big, pin, bin, sit)

**Objective:** Child reads five words with short i.
**Materials:** Word cards: pig, big, pin, bin, sit. Picture cards: pig, pin, bin. Action card: sit. Worksheet W21.

- **Warm-up (3):** "Short sound": teacher says "i" (as in "ink"), children say "ih" and show a small gap with thumb and finger.
- **Discover (5):** "P-i-g, pig. b-i-g, big. p-i-n, pin." Hindi: "i की आवाज़ छोटी होती है, जैसे ink में।"
- **Practise (7):** Pairs: one child reads the word, the other finds the picture.
- **Apply (6):** Worksheet W21: match words to pictures.
- **Check (3):** "Read: bin." "Read: sit." "Which one is big?" Record.
- **Celebrate (2)**
- **Recall:** Show pig card. "Read it."

### Lesson 22: Short o (dog, hot, pot, mop, box)

**Objective:** Child reads five words with short o.
**Materials:** Word cards: dog, hot, pot, mop, box. Picture cards: dog, pot, mop, box. Worksheet W22.

- **Warm-up (3):** "Short o": children make an "o" shape with their mouth and say "ah-ah" quickly.
- **Discover (5):** "D-o-g, dog. h-o-t, hot. p-o-t, pot." Hindi: "o की आवाज़ जैसे hot में।"
- **Practise (7):** Pairs read to each other, swap.
- **Apply (6):** Worksheet W22: draw a line from word to picture.
- **Check (3):** "Read: mop." "Read: hot." "Which is a box?" Record.
- **Celebrate (2)**
- **Recall:** Show dog card. "Read it."

### Lesson 23: Short u (sun, cup, bus, bug, mug)

**Objective:** Child reads five words with short u.
**Materials:** Word cards: sun, cup, bus, bug, mug. Picture cards for each. Worksheet W23.

- **Warm-up (3):** "Umbrella sound": children open an imaginary umbrella and say "uh".
- **Discover (5):** "S-u-n, sun. c-u-p, cup. b-u-s, bus." Hindi: "u की आवाज़ जैसे umbrella में।"
- **Practise (7):** Teacher reads a word, children find the picture among 3.
- **Apply (6):** Worksheet W23: circle the word that matches each picture.
- **Check (3):** "Read: bug." "Read: mug." "Which word has the u sound?" Record.
- **Celebrate (2)**
- **Recall:** Show bus card. "Read it."

### Lesson 24: Mixed vowels

**Objective:** Child picks the right vowel to make a word.
**Materials:** Picture cards: cat, pig, dog, cup, bed (or bag). Vowel cards a, i, o, u. Worksheet W24.

- **Warm-up (3):** "Vowel hunt": teacher says a vowel sound, children show the vowel card.
- **Discover (5):** "This is c-?-t. Cat has a. Cup has u." Show the picture, children say the missing vowel.
- **Practise (7):** Pairs: one child says a picture word, the other builds it with letter cards (missing vowel left out).
- **Apply (6):** Worksheet W24: fill in the missing vowel (c_t, p_g, d_g, c_p).
- **Check (3):** "Which vowel is in pig?" "Which vowel is in dog?" Record.
- **Celebrate (2)**
- **Recall:** Show "hot" card. "What vowel?"

### Lesson 25: Week 5 review and mini-check

**Objective:** Child reads 15+ CVC words.
**Materials:** Word cards (all CVC so far). Worksheet W25.

- **Warm-up (3):** Chant the vowels: a, e, i, o, u.
- **Discover (5):** Review: show word cards, children read.
- **Practise (7):** Read-around: each child reads 2 words.
- **Apply (6):** Worksheet W25: read 6 words and draw the picture.
- **Check (3):** **Week 5 mini-check** (15 CVC words, read aloud or point to picture). Record.
- **Celebrate (2)**
- **Stuck-child check:** Apply the stuck-child rule.

## Week 6: Things around me

### Lesson 26: Body parts (head, hand, leg, eye, nose, mouth, ear)

**Objective:** Child names 7 body parts and points to them.
**Materials:** Word cards and picture cards for each. Worksheet W26.

- **Warm-up (3):** "Simon says": "Touch your head." "Touch your nose."
- **Discover (5):** Show each card. "Head. सिर. Head." Repeat for the others.
- **Practise (7):** Pairs: one child names a body part, the other touches it on their own body.
- **Apply (6):** Worksheet W26: label the picture of a child.
- **Check (3):** "Point to your ear." "What is this?" (show hand). Record.
- **Celebrate (2)**
- **Recall:** Show leg card. "What is this?"

### Lesson 27: Family (mother, father, sister, brother, baby, grandmother)

**Objective:** Child names 6 family members.
**Materials:** Picture cards: family members. Worksheet W27.

- **Warm-up (3):** "Who is this?" Teacher shows a card, children say the word.
- **Discover (5):** "Mother. माँ. Mother." Teach family words in a short story: "This is my mother. This is my father."
- **Practise (7):** Pairs: one child points to a card and says "This is my ___." The other responds "Good."
- **Apply (6):** Worksheet W27: match family words to pictures.
- **Check (3):** "Who is this?" (show grandmother card). Record.
- **Celebrate (2)**
- **Recall:** Show baby card. "Who is this?"

### Lesson 28: Colours (red, blue, green, yellow, black, white)

**Objective:** Child names 6 colours.
**Materials:** Colour cards (solid colour squares with word). Coloured objects if available. Worksheet W28.

- **Warm-up (3):** "Colour hunt": teacher says a colour, children touch something that colour in the room.
- **Discover (5):** "Red. लाल. Red. Blue. नीला. Blue." Repeat each.
- **Practise (7):** Pairs: one child shows a colour card, the other says the name.
- **Apply (6):** Worksheet W28: colour the picture as the word says.
- **Check (3):** "What colour is this?" (show red). Record.
- **Celebrate (2)**
- **Recall:** Show green card. "What colour?"

### Lesson 29: Fruits (apple, banana, mango, orange, grapes)

**Objective:** Child names 5 fruits.
**Materials:** Picture cards: apple, banana, mango, orange, grapes. Real fruit if available. Worksheet W29.

- **Warm-up (3):** "Fruit basket": children pass an imaginary fruit around the circle.
- **Discover (5):** "Banana. केला. Banana." Use real fruit where possible.
- **Practise (7):** Pairs: one child shows the card, the other says the word and its first sound.
- **Apply (6):** Worksheet W29: match fruit to the word.
- **Check (3):** "What is this?" (show mango). Record.
- **Celebrate (2)**
- **Recall:** Show grapes card. "What is this?"

### Lesson 30: Classroom objects (book, pen, desk, chair, board, bag)

**Objective:** Child names 6 classroom objects and uses "This is a ___."
**Materials:** Picture cards or real objects: book, pen, desk, chair, board, bag. Sentence strips. Worksheet W30.

- **Warm-up (3):** Teacher points to an object. Children say "This is a ___."
- **Discover (5):** Model: "This is a book. This is a pen." Hindi: "यह एक ___ है।"
- **Practise (7):** Pairs: one child points to an object and says the sentence. The other agrees.
- **Apply (6):** Worksheet W30: label the classroom picture.
- **Check (3):** "What is this?" (show chair). Speaking check: 2 children answer "What is this?" with a full sentence. Record.
- **Celebrate (2)**
- **Recall:** Start of Lesson 31: show board card.

## Week 7: Doing and saying

### Lesson 31: Action words (run, jump, sit)

**Objective:** Child reads and acts out 3 action words, and says "I can ___."
**Materials:** Word cards: run, jump, sit. Action picture cards. Sentence strips: "I can run." Worksheet W31.

- **Warm-up (3):** Teacher calls out an action. Children do it.
- **Discover (5):** Show each word and picture. "Run. दौड़ो. Run." Act it out.
- **Practise (7):** Pairs: one child reads an action word, the other acts it out.
- **Apply (6):** Sentence strip: "I can run." Children read and act.
- **Check (3):** "Read: jump." "Say: I can sit." Record.
- **Celebrate (2)**
- **Recall:** Show run card. "Read it."

### Lesson 32: Action words (eat, drink, sleep)

**Objective:** Child reads and acts out 3 more action words.
**Materials:** Word cards: eat, drink, sleep. Picture cards. Worksheet W32.

- **Warm-up (3):** "Mime game": teacher mimes an action, children guess.
- **Discover (5):** "Eat. खाओ. Eat. Drink. पियो. Drink. Sleep. सोओ. Sleep."
- **Practise (7):** Pairs: one child reads and the other acts.
- **Apply (6):** Worksheet W32: match word to picture.
- **Check (3):** "Read: drink." "Say: I can eat." Record.
- **Celebrate (2)**
- **Recall:** Show sleep card. "Read it."

### Lesson 33: Action words (read, write, draw, sing)

**Objective:** Child reads and says 4 more action words.
**Materials:** Word cards: read, write, draw, sing. Picture cards. Worksheet W33.

- **Warm-up (3):** Clap the beat, children sing "la la la".
- **Discover (5):** Show each word and picture. Act out.
- **Practise (7):** Pairs.
- **Apply (6):** Worksheet W33: match words.
- **Check (3):** "Read: write." "Say: I can draw." Record.
- **Celebrate (2)**
- **Recall:** Show sing card. "Read it."

### Lesson 34: "I can ___" sentences

**Objective:** Child reads and says "I can ___" with 3 action words.
**Materials:** Sentence strips: "I can run." "I can jump." "I can read." Worksheet W34.

- **Warm-up (3):** Children say "I can clap."
- **Discover (5):** Model: "I can run." Read the sentence strip. Point to each word.
- **Practise (7):** Pairs: one child says "I can ___" with an action, the other says it back.
- **Apply (6):** Worksheet W34: read 3 sentences and draw a line to each picture.
- **Check (3):** Speaking check: 2 children say a "I can ___" sentence. Record.
- **Celebrate (2)**
- **Recall:** Start of Lesson 35: read "I can sing."

### Lesson 35: Questions: "What is this?" and "Can you ___?"

**Objective:** Child asks and answers 2 simple questions in English.
**Materials:** Picture cards. Sentence strips: "What is this? It is a ___." "Can you ___? Yes, I can." Worksheet W35.

- **Warm-up (3):** Teacher asks "What is your name?" Children answer.
- **Discover (5):** Model both questions and answers. Hindi: "सवाल पूछो, जवाब दो।"
- **Practise (7):** Pairs: one asks "What is this?" with a picture, the other answers.
- **Apply (6):** Pairs: one asks "Can you run?", the other answers "Yes, I can."
- **Check (3):** Speaking check: 2 children answer "What is this?" with a full sentence. Record.
- **Celebrate (2)**
- **Recall:** Start of Lesson 36: ask "What is this?" with a picture.

## Week 8: Show what I know

### Lesson 36: Revision: reading (CVC and sight words)

**Objective:** Child reads CVC words and sight words without help.
**Materials:** Word cards (all CVC and sight words). Worksheet W36.

- **Warm-up (3):** Quick flash card game with sight words.
- **Discover (5):** Quick model: sound out one word from each set.
- **Practise (7):** Pairs read to each other, teacher listens.
- **Apply (6):** Worksheet W36: read 10 words and draw the picture.
- **Check (3):** Teacher listens to 3 children read 5 words. Record.
- **Celebrate (2)**
- **Recall:** Not applicable (revision).

### Lesson 37: Revision: writing (words and sentences)

**Objective:** Child writes 5 words from dictation and 1 sentence.
**Materials:** Whiteboard, pencils, paper. Worksheet W37.

- **Warm-up (3):** Trace a letter in the air.
- **Discover (5):** Model: teacher says "cat." Child writes c-a-t on paper.
- **Practise (7):** Pairs: one says a word, the other writes it. Swap.
- **Apply (6):** Worksheet W37: write 1 sentence about a picture ("I see a ___.")
- **Check (3):** Dictation of 3 words (cat, sun, pen). Record.
- **Celebrate (2)**
- **Recall:** Not applicable.

### Lesson 38: Listening and comprehension

**Objective:** Child listens to a 2-sentence story and answers 2 questions by pointing.
**Materials:** Story strip (see W38). Picture cards for answers. Worksheet W38.

- **Warm-up (3):** "Listen and point": teacher says an action, children point to the picture.
- **Discover (5):** Read the story aloud, slowly: "I have a cat. The cat is red." Show the picture.
- **Practise (7):** Ask: "What do I have?" (point to cat). "What colour is the cat?" (point to red).
- **Apply (6):** Pairs: one child reads a new story, the other answers with pictures.
- **Check (3):** Teacher reads 2-sentence story. Child answers 2 questions. Record.
- **Celebrate (2)**
- **Recall:** Not applicable.

### Lesson 39: Speaking practice (role play)

**Objective:** Child takes part in a 2–3 turn English conversation.
**Materials:** Role-play cards: "What is your name?" "My name is ___." "How are you?" "I am fine." Worksheet W39.

- **Warm-up (3):** Teacher waves and says "Hello. How are you?" Children answer.
- **Discover (5):** Model a short dialogue with a child.
- **Practise (7):** Pairs: practise the dialogue on the role-play card.
- **Apply (6):** Pairs act out a shop: "What is this?" "It is a ___." "Can I have it?"
- **Check (3):** **Speaking check** for the whole class (2 min each child, over the week). Record scores on the speaking column.
- **Celebrate (2)**
- **Recall:** Not applicable.

### Lesson 40: Endline day

**Objective:** Measure each child's endline. See `03_assessments.md`, Part B.
**Materials:** Endline test sheets, tracking sheet, stickers.

- **Warm-up (3):** Calm, quiet start. "Today we show what we know."
- **Discover (5):** Explain the endline in simple terms: "You will do some tasks. Do your best. It is okay to try."
- **Practise (7):** Run the endline in small groups (see `03_assessments.md` for timing). The rest of the class does Worksheet W40 (match words to pictures) quietly.
- **Apply (6):** Speaking part: 1:1 with the teacher, 2 min per child. Others continue the worksheet.
- **Check (3):** Teacher records all scores on the tracking sheet.
- **Celebrate (5):** Big celebration. Each child gets a certificate ("I learned ___"). Use the class sticker chart.
- **After the course:** Note the date for the delayed check (2 weeks later, see Part C).
