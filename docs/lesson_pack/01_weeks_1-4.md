# Lessons 1–20 (Weeks 1–4)

Each lesson follows the daily routine in `00_README.md`. Hindi lines are drafts for the Hindi-speaking teacher to check.

Common materials for every lesson: the letter, picture or word cards named in the lesson, the tracking sheet, stickers.

---

## Week 1: Letters A–M

### Lesson 1: Letters A, B, C

**Objective:** The child says the name and sound of a, b and c, and points to each one when asked.
**Materials:** Letter cards A/a, B/b, C/c. Picture cards: apple, ball, cat. Worksheet W1.

- **Warm-up (3):** "Touch your nose. Clap twice." Then "Show me your hands."
- **Discover (5):** Hold up A. "This is A. A. Apple. Ahh." Hindi: "यह A है। A से Apple।" Say it with me: "A." Repeat for B (ball) and C (cat).
- **Practise (7):** Teacher says a sound, children hold up the letter. "Show me /b/." Then pairs take turns showing each other.
- **Apply (6):** Teacher holds up apple, ball and cat. "Which one starts with A?" Children point. Repeat with B and C.
- **Check (3):** "Point to C." "What sound does B make?" "Which starts with C: cat, dog or sun?"
- **Celebrate (2):** Sticker for the best effort.
- **Recall (next day, 1 min):** Show a ball card. "What sound?" Child says /b/.

### Lesson 2: Letters D, E, F

**Objective:** Name and sound of d, e, f.
**Materials:** Letter cards D/d, E/e, F/f. Picture cards: dog, egg, fish. Worksheet W2.

- **Warm-up (3):** "Stand up, sit down" action game with commands in English.
- **Discover (5):** "D. Dog. Dah." Hindi: "D से Dog, कुत्ता।" Repeat for E (egg, "eh", अंडा) and F (fish, "ff", मछली).
- **Practise (7):** Teacher says "Show me /d/." Children hold up the letter. Then "Say the sound and point to the card."
- **Apply (6):** Dog, egg, fish cards are laid face down. Child picks one, says the word and first letter.
- **Check (3):** "What starts with D?" (dog/cat/sun) "Point to E." "What sound does F make?"
- **Celebrate (2)**
- **Recall (next day):** Show egg card. "What sound does it start with?"

### Lesson 3: Letters G, H, I

**Objective:** Name and sound of g, h, i.
**Materials:** Letter cards G/g, H/h, I/i. Picture cards: goat, hat, insect. Worksheet W3.

- **Warm-up (3):** "Hands on head, hands on knees." Repeat faster.
- **Discover (5):** "G. Goat. Guh." Hindi: "G से Goat, बकरी।" "H. Hat. Huh." (टोपी). "I. Insect. Ih." (कीड़ा). Explain: I sounds like "ih", as in "sit".
- **Practise (7):** Teacher says a sound, children show the letter. Then pairs quiz each other.
- **Apply (6):** Show goat, hat, insect. "Which one starts with H?" Children point.
- **Check (3):** "What does G say?" "Point to I." "Which starts with H: hat or cup?"
- **Celebrate (2)**
- **Recall (next day):** Show hat card. "What sound?"

### Lesson 4: Letters J, K, L, M

**Objective:** Name and sound of j, k, l, m.
**Materials:** Letter cards J/j, K/k, L/l, M/m. Picture cards: jug, kite, lion, mango. Worksheet W4.

- **Warm-up (3):** Teacher says a letter sound. Children do a jumping jack for the sound /j/, clap for /k/ and so on.
- **Discover (5):** "J. Jug. Juh." (जग). "K. Kite. Kuh." (पतंग). "L. Lion. Luh." (शेर). "M. Mango. Mmm." (आम). Repeat each with children.
- **Practise (7):** Pairs: one says the sound, the other shows the letter. Swap.
- **Apply (6):** Lay out jug, kite, lion, mango. Teacher says "Find the one that starts with /l/." Children pick.
- **Check (3):** "Point to K." "What sound does M make?" "Which starts with J: jug or ball?"
- **Celebrate (2)**
- **Recall (next day):** Show lion card. "What sound?"

### Lesson 5: Review A–M

**Objective:** Child can name and sound out all letters A–M.
**Materials:** Letter cards A–M (mixed). Worksheet W5 (letter match).

- **Warm-up (3):** Sing or chant the alphabet A to M together.
- **Discover (5):** Quick review. Teacher shows a card, children say the letter name and sound together.
- **Practise (7):** "Letter detective." Teacher places 5 cards on the table. Says a sound. Children find it.
- **Apply (6):** Worksheet W5: match letters to pictures (apple, ball, cat, dog, egg, fish, goat, hat, insect, jug, kite, lion, mango).
- **Check (3):** Teacher asks 5 letters at random. Speaking check: 2 children say their favourite letter and its word.
- **Celebrate (2)**
- **Recall:** At start of Lesson 6, show 3 letters from A–M without warning.

## Week 2: Letters N–Z

### Lesson 6: Letters N, O, P

**Objective:** Name and sound of n, o, p.
**Materials:** Letter cards N/n, O/o, P/p. Picture cards: nest, octopus, pen. Worksheet W6.

- **Warm-up (3):** "Simon says": act out the first sound of a word the teacher names.
- **Discover (5):** "N. Nest. Nnn." (घोंसला). "O. Octopus. Ah." (ऑक्टोपस). Explain: O is short, like "ot" in "hot". "P. Pen. Puh." (पेन).
- **Practise (7):** Pairs take turns: one holds up a card, the other says the sound.
- **Apply (6):** Show nest, octopus, pen. "Which one ends with N?" Hint: nest ends with /t/, pen ends with /n/. Children pick pen.
- **Check (3):** "What does O say?" "Point to P." "Which starts with N?"
- **Celebrate (2)**
- **Recall:** Show pen card. "What sound at the end?"

### Lesson 7: Letters Q, R, S

**Objective:** Name and sound of q, r, s.
**Materials:** Letter cards Q/q, R/r, S/s. Picture cards: queen (or "queen" card), rabbit, sun. Worksheet W7.

- **Warm-up (3):** Hop like a rabbit, sit like a queen.
- **Discover (5):** "Q. Queen. Kw." (रानी). Explain Q is always followed by u. "R. Rabbit. Rrr." (खरगोश). "S. Sun. Sss." (सूरज).
- **Practise (7):** Teacher says a sound, children show the letter card. Pairs quiz.
- **Apply (6):** Show queen, rabbit, sun. "Which starts with R?" Children point.
- **Check (3):** "What does Q say?" "Point to S." "What sound does R make?"
- **Celebrate (2)**
- **Recall:** Show sun card. "What sound?"

### Lesson 8: Letters T, U, V

**Objective:** Name and sound of t, u, v.
**Materials:** Letter cards T/t, U/u, V/v. Picture cards: tiger, umbrella, van. Worksheet W8.

- **Warm-up (3):** "Tiger walk" around the room, then freeze.
- **Discover (5):** "T. Tiger. Tuh." (बाघ). "U. Umbrella. Uh." (छाता). Explain: U is short, like "up". "V. Van. Vvv." (वैन).
- **Practise (7):** Pairs. One child says a word from the picture cards, the other says the first sound.
- **Apply (6):** Show tiger, umbrella, van. "Which one has the short U sound?" Children choose umbrella.
- **Check (3):** "What sound does T make?" "Point to V." "Which starts with U?"
- **Celebrate (2)**
- **Recall:** Show van card. "What sound?"

### Lesson 9: Letters W, X, Y, Z

**Objective:** Name and sound of w, x, y, z.
**Materials:** Letter cards W/w, X/x, Y/y, Z/z. Picture cards: watch, box, yellow (colour card), zip. Worksheet W9.

- **Warm-up (3):** Shake like a zip sound: "zzz".
- **Discover (5):** "W. Watch. Wuh." (घड़ी). "X. Box. Ks." (डिब्बा). Explain: X sounds like "ks" at the end of box. "Y. Yellow. Yuh." (पीला). "Z. Zip. Zzz." (ज़िप).
- **Practise (7):** Teacher holds up a letter, children say the sound and word.
- **Apply (6):** Lay out watch, box, yellow, zip. "Which one ends with the X sound?" Children pick box.
- **Check (3):** "What does W say?" "Point to Z." "Which starts with Y?"
- **Celebrate (2)**
- **Recall:** Show box card. "What sound at the end?"

### Lesson 10: Review all 26 letters

**Objective:** Child names and sounds all 26 letters (capital and small).
**Materials:** Full letter card set. Worksheet W10 (letter match, capital to small).

- **Warm-up (3):** Alphabet chant, slow, then fast.
- **Discover (5):** Teacher shows a random card. Children say name and sound.
- **Practise (7):** "Letter race" in pairs: one child says the letters they see, the other checks.
- **Apply (6):** Worksheet W10: match capital letter to small letter.
- **Check (3):** Full letter check: 10 letters picked at random by teacher. **Record each child's score on the tracking sheet.** This is the Week 2 mini-check.
- **Celebrate (2)**
- **Recall:** Start of Lesson 11, show 5 random letters.

## Week 3: Sounds in words

### Lesson 11: Beginning sounds (/k/)

**Objective:** Child hears the first sound in a word and picks the matching picture.
**Materials:** Picture cards: cat, kite, cup, king, dog, sun. Worksheet W11.

- **Warm-up (3):** "Hear and clap": teacher says a word, children clap if it starts with /k/.
- **Discover (5):** Say "cat. Cat starts with /k/." "Kite starts with /k/ too. Cup starts with /k/. Cat, kite, cup." Hindi: "Cat, kite, cup, सब /k/ से शुरू होते हैं।"
- **Practise (7):** Teacher says words. Children clap for /k/, stay still for others.
- **Apply (6):** Lay out 6 cards. "Which starts with /k/?" Children put them in a pile.
- **Check (3):** Teacher says 3 words. Children hold up 1 finger if it starts with /k/, 2 if not.
- **Celebrate (2)**
- **Recall:** Show king card. "What sound?"

### Lesson 12: Beginning sounds (b, d, f, g, h, m)

**Objective:** Child picks the picture that starts with a given sound.
**Materials:** Picture cards: ball, dog, fish, goat, hat, mango, bag, pen, sun, cup. Worksheet W12.

- **Warm-up (3):** "Sound of the day": teacher picks one sound, children tap their knee every time they hear it.
- **Discover (5):** Teacher says "Ball, bag, bat." "They all start with /b/." Repeat for dog and fish, goat and hat, mango and milk (use a mango card).
- **Practise (7):** Teacher says "Find the one that starts with /f/." Children point from 3 cards.
- **Apply (6):** Pairs: one child says a word, the other holds up the card that starts with the same sound.
- **Check (3):** "Which starts with /h/: hat, sun or cup?" "Which starts with /g/: goat, dog or box?" Record scores.
- **Celebrate (2)**
- **Recall:** Show mango card. "What sound?"

### Lesson 13: Ending sounds

**Objective:** Child hears the last sound in a word.
**Materials:** Picture cards: cat, sun, cup, bag, pen, hat, bus, mug, sock. Worksheet W13.

- **Warm-up (3):** "Say it backwards": teacher says "tap" and children say "pat".
- **Discover (5):** "Cat. Cat ends with /t/. Sun ends with /n/. Cup ends with /p/. Say it with me: cat, /t/." Hindi: "अंत में आवाज़ सुनो।"
- **Practise (7):** Teacher says a word. Children tap their shoulder for the last sound and say it.
- **Apply (6):** Lay out cat, sun, cup, bag. "Which one ends with /n/?" Children pick sun.
- **Check (3):** "What sound does hat end with?" "Which ends with /g/: bag or cup?" Record scores.
- **Celebrate (2)**
- **Recall:** Show pen card. "What sound at the end?"

### Lesson 14: Sound counting

**Objective:** Child can count the sounds in a 3-sound word.
**Materials:** Picture cards: cat, dog, sun, pen, bag, cup. Counting chips or stones (3 per child). Worksheet W14.

- **Warm-up (3):** Clap the syllables in the children's names.
- **Discover (5):** Teacher says "cat": "/k/ — /æ/ — /t/. Three sounds." Put one chip down for each sound. Repeat for dog.
- **Practise (7):** Children say the word slowly and put one chip per sound.
- **Apply (6):** Teacher shows a picture. Children count the sounds and show with fingers.
- **Check (3):** "How many sounds in sun?" "In pen?" "In cup?" Record scores.
- **Celebrate (2)**
- **Recall:** Show bag card. "How many sounds?"

### Lesson 15: Week 3 review

**Objective:** Child hears beginning and ending sounds and counts sounds.
**Materials:** Mixed picture cards (20). Worksheet W15.

- **Warm-up (3):** "Sound game": teacher says a sound, children name a word that starts with it.
- **Discover (5):** Quick review of the three skills from Lessons 11–14.
- **Practise (7):** Mixed card game: teacher says "Find the one that starts with /b/ and ends with /t/" (bat, ball, bug, cat — use bat).
- **Apply (6):** Worksheet W15: circle the picture that starts with the given sound.
- **Check (3):** Speaking check: 2 children say a word that starts with /s/. Record scores.
- **Celebrate (2)**
- **Recall:** Start of Lesson 16: show cat card, ask beginning sound.

## Week 4: Short a words (c-a-t)

### Lesson 16: Blending c-a-t

**Objective:** Child blends three sounds into a word and reads CAT.
**Materials:** Letter cards c, a, t. Word card CAT. Picture card: cat. Worksheet W16.

- **Warm-up (3):** "Robot talk": teacher says "c-a-t" slowly, children say "cat" fast.
- **Discover (5):** Show cards c, a, t. "This is /k/, /æ/, /t/. Put them together: cat." Hindi: "सबको जोड़ो, cat।" Show picture of cat. "Cat. बिल्ली."
- **Practise (7):** Pairs: one child slides their finger under the letters while saying the sounds. The other says the word.
- **Apply (6):** Sentence: "I see a cat." Children read the sentence strip with teacher.
- **Check (3):** Show word card CAT. "Read this word." Record.
- **Celebrate (2)**
- **Recall:** Show word card CAT on the board. "Read it again."

### Lesson 17: Short a words (bat, hat, mat, sat, rat)

**Objective:** Child reads five -at words.
**Materials:** Word cards: bat, hat, mat, sat, rat. Picture cards for each. Worksheet W17.

- **Warm-up (3):** "Sounds dance": teacher says sounds, children stamp for each.
- **Discover (5):** Show "at" as a chunk: "a-t, at. Now put a letter in front: b-at, bat. h-at, hat." Hindi: "पहले at, फिर पहला अक्षर।"
- **Practise (7):** Teacher points to a word, children read. Then pairs read to each other.
- **Apply (6):** Match word card to picture card (bat, hat, mat, sat is an action so use "sat" with a picture of a child sitting).
- **Check (3):** "Read: hat." "Read: rat." "Which one is mat?" Record.
- **Celebrate (2)**
- **Recall:** Show "mat" card. "Read it."

### Lesson 18: -an words (can, fan, man, van, pan)

**Objective:** Child reads five -an words.
**Materials:** Word cards: can, fan, man, van, pan. Picture cards for each. Worksheet W18.

- **Warm-up (3):** "Hop on the sound": teacher says "an", children hop.
- **Discover (5):** "A-n, an. f-an, fan. v-an, van." Show each picture.
- **Practise (7):** Children read the words aloud. Then "Find the word that matches the picture" in pairs.
- **Apply (6):** Sentence strip: "The fan is on." Children read with teacher.
- **Check (3):** "Read: pan." "Which is a man?" Record.
- **Celebrate (2)**
- **Recall:** Show van card. "Read it."

### Lesson 19: Sight words (I, a, the, is, see)

**Objective:** Child recognises and reads five high-frequency words.
**Materials:** Sight word cards: I, a, the, is, see. Worksheet W19.

- **Warm-up (3):** Flash card game: teacher holds up a sight word for 2 seconds.
- **Discover (5):** Each word: teacher says it, spells it, says it again. "I. This is I. It is a short word." Hindi: "ये शब्द याद करने हैं।"
- **Practise (7):** Pairs: one shows the word card, other reads it. Swap.
- **Apply (6):** Sentence strip: "I see a cat." Children point to each word as they read.
- **Check (3):** Teacher shows 3 sight words. Children read each. Record.
- **Celebrate (2)**
- **Recall:** Start of Lesson 20: show "the" and "is".

### Lesson 20: Week 4 review and mini-check

**Objective:** Child reads CVC -a words and sight words from memory.
**Materials:** Word cards (c-a-t set, -at set, -an set, sight words). Picture cards. Worksheet W20.

- **Warm-up (3):** Chant: "c-a-t, cat! b-a-t, bat!"
- **Discover (5):** Review: show each word card, children read.
- **Practise (7):** Read-around: children take turns reading one word each.
- **Apply (6):** Worksheet W20: read the words and draw a line to the matching picture.
- **Check (3):** **Week 4 mini-check** (10 items): 5 CVC words, 4 sight words, 1 sentence. Record on the tracking sheet.
- **Celebrate (2)**
- **Stuck-child check:** Any child under 50%: apply the stuck-child rule (see README section 8).
