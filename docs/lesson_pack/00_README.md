# English Foundations: 8-Week Lesson Pack

**Who it's for:** Pre-emergent children in Grades 3–5 (about 30 children, one class).
**Length:** 8 weeks, 5 lessons a week, 40 lessons, plus baseline and endline.
**Time:** 30 minutes a day, in class, taught by the teacher. No phones needed.
**Teacher:** English-speaking, also speaks Hindi. Hindi is used only for explanations.

---

## 1. The goal in plain words

By the end of 8 weeks:
- Every child is measured on five skills at the start and the end.
- **Target:** most children move up one level (Pre-emergent → Emergent), and each child improves in at least 3 of the 5 skills.
- The children should be able to do things at the end that they could not do on Day 1: read CVC words, write words from dictation, answer simple questions in English.
- A delayed check 2 weeks after the course confirms the gains last. (This is outside the 8 weeks.)

---

## 2. Language rule (important)

Use this pattern for every new word:

1. Say the **English** word clearly.
2. Give the **Hindi** meaning or explanation.
3. Say the **English** word again, and have the children repeat it.

Example: "Cat. बिल्ली। Cat. Say it with me: cat."

Never translate whole English sentences into Hindi during tests. Tests are English only. The child may point or nod.

Hindi lines in these lesson plans are drafts. **The Hindi-speaking teacher should check them before Week 1.**

---

## 3. Daily routine (30 minutes)

| Stage | Min | What the teacher does |
|---|---|---|
| Warm-up | 3 | Quick action game and revision of yesterday's item |
| Discover | 5 | Introduce the new item with a card or picture. Model it. |
| Practise | 7 | Whole class says it, then pairs practise, then the teacher checks a few children |
| Apply | 6 | Children use the item in a new picture or sentence |
| Check | 3 | 3 quick questions. Teacher notes results on the tracking sheet. |
| Celebrate | 2 | Sticker or star. Name one thing each child did well. |
| Buffer | 4 | Catch-up, repeat if needed |

**Speaking:** Every day, during Check, the teacher listens to the 6 children on today's speaking rotation (about 1 minute each). Every child is heard once a week, so each child gets about 8 speaking checks plus the endline. Wherever a lesson says "2 children speak," read it as "today's speaking rotation." (See section 5.)

---

## 4. Grouping and classroom set-up

- **Whole class:** Discover and Celebrate.
- **Pairs (buddies):** Practise and Apply. Change pairs each week.
- **Small group (3 groups of 10):** Check. While the teacher checks one group, the other two groups do a worksheet from the pack.
- Keep a sticker chart on the wall. Use stars for effort, not for test scores.

---

## 5. Speaking checks (plan this carefully)

With 30 children, the rotation is 6 children a day, Monday to Friday, so every child is heard once a week:
- Mon: children 1–6 · Tue: 7–12 · Wed: 13–18 · Thu: 19–24 · Fri: 25–30 (use the tracking sheet order)
- About 1 minute per child, during Check.
- Use the same 5 speaking questions each week, from the speaking card set (section 5 of `04_cards_worksheets.md`).
- Score each answer 0, 1 or 2 on the tracking sheet (see the assessment file).

## 8. Using the pack

1. **Day 1 (before Lesson 1):** Run the baseline (`03_assessments.md`, Part A). Record scores on the tracking sheet.
2. **Each lesson:** Follow the lesson plan in `01_weeks_1-4.md` or `02_weeks_5-8.md`.
3. **Each Check:** Record results on the tracking sheet the same day.
4. **End of Week 4 and Week 8:** Look at the tracking sheet. Children who are stuck get a 5-minute catch-up in the Buffer time for the next week (see the stuck-child rules below).
5. **Day 40:** Run the endline (`03_assessments.md`, Part B).
6. **2 weeks after the course:** Run the delayed check (Part C).

### Stuck-child rules

- Missed 2 Checks in a row, or under 50% on a skill for 2 weeks: move the child to a buddy pair with a stronger partner for that skill, and repeat the last lesson's Practise activity for 5 minutes.
- Still stuck after 1 more week: flag on the tracking sheet and tell the head teacher. The child may need smaller steps, not more of the same.
- Never make a child read aloud in front of the class if they are visibly anxious. Let them whisper the answer to you, or point.

### Tone rules

- Use "Try again" or "Let's look again" when a child is wrong. Never say "wrong" loudly and never use red ink.
- Do not rank children in front of others.
- Praise effort and the specific skill: "You remembered the sound of B!"
