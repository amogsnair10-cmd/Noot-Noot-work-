# Cards and Worksheets

Print and laminate the cards if you can. Make two copies of letter cards.

---

## 1. Letter cards

- A–Z, capital and small, one card each. Two full sets (52 cards).

## 5. Speaking question set (use every week, during Check)

**Week 1–4:**
1. What is your name?
2. How are you?
3. What is this? (show a card)
4. What colour is this?
5. Can you ___? (act: "Can you jump?")

**Week 5–8:**
1. What is your name?
2. Where do you live?
3. What do you like to eat?
4. Can you ___? (act: "Can you run?")
5. What is this? (show a card)

Score each answer on the tracking sheet: 0, 1 or 2.

## 6. Worksheet list

Each worksheet is a simple one-page task. You can make them by hand or on a computer. Use large, clear pictures and lots of space for tracing.

| Code | Lesson | Worksheet | Task |
|---|---|---|---|
| W1 | 1 | Letters A, B, C | Trace A, B, C (capital and small). Circle the picture that starts with each letter. |
| W2 | 2 | Letters D, E, F | Trace D, E, F. Draw a line from each letter to its picture (dog, egg, fish). |
| W3 | 3 | Letters G, H, I | Trace G, H, I. Circle the picture for each letter (goat, hat, insect). |
| W4 | 4 | Letters J, K, L, M | Trace J, K, L, M. Match to picture (jug, kite, lion, mango). |
| W5 | 5 | A–M review | Match 13 letters to 13 pictures. |
| W6 | 6 | Letters N, O, P | Trace N, O, P. Match to picture (nest, octopus, pen). |
| W7 | 7 | Letters Q, R, S | Trace Q, R, S. Match to picture (queen, rabbit, sun). |
| W8 | 8 | Letters T, U, V | Trace T, U, V. Match to picture (tiger, umbrella, van). |
| W9 | 9 | Letters W, X, Y, Z | Trace W, X, Y, Z. Match to picture (watch, box, yellow, zip). |
| W10 | 10 | All 26 letters | Match capital to small letters (26 lines). |
| W11 | 11 | Beginning /k/ | Circle the pictures that start with /k/ (cat, kite, cup, dog, sun, king). |
| W12 | 12 | Beginning sounds | Circle the picture that starts with the sound the teacher says (b, d, f, g, h, m). |
| W13 | 13 | Ending sounds | Circle the picture that ends with the sound (t, n, p, g). |
| W14 | 14 | Counting sounds | Draw one dot per sound in cat, dog, sun, pen, bag, cup. |
| W15 | 15 | Week 3 review | Circle the picture that starts or ends with the sound given. |

| W33 | 33 | Action words 3 | Match read, write, draw, sing to pictures. |
| W34 | 34 | I can sentences | Read 3 sentences and draw a line to each picture. |
| W35 | 35 | Questions | Read "What is this?" and "Can you ___?" and answer with a picture. |
| W36 | 36 | Reading revision | Read 10 words and draw the picture. |
| W37 | 37 | Writing revision | Write one sentence: "I see a ___." |
| W38 | 38 | Story | Listen to the story "I have a dog. The dog is big." Circle the pictures. |
| W39 | 39 | Speaking role play | Role-play cards (see section 7). |

## 7. Role-play cards (Lesson 39)

- Card A: "Hello. What is your name?" / "My name is ___."
- Card B: "How are you?" / "I am fine. Thank you."
- Card C (shop): "What is this?" / "It is a ___." / "Can I have it?" / "Yes, you can."

---

## 8. Stickers and certificates

- Sticker chart: stars for effort, stickers for finishing a worksheet. Never award stars for "fastest".
- Endline certificate: "I learned ___" with the child's name. Fill in one new word or skill per child.
