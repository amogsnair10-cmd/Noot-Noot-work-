# Student Council Leadership Programme

**Sardar Kanhoji Angre School (PMC), Std 6-7 student council, 12 members.** A training programme that builds seven leadership skills, turns them into real duties (weekly council meetings, assemblies, late-comers register, reporting to teachers, no violence), and measures the change with a baseline and endline.

## The problem

Indiscipline, a lack of structure, frequent fights, late arrivals, students who do not respect or listen to the council, and a council with no clear understanding of its roles or how to perform them.

## Leadership skills and modules

Skills: L1 Role Ownership, L2 Leading by Example, L3 Clear Communication, L4 Calm Conflict Handling, L5 Empathy and Fairness, L6 Teamwork and Problem-Solving, L7 Planning and Follow-through.

Modules: M1 Who is a leader? (S1-2), M2 Lead by example (S3), M3 Speak and listen (S4), M4 Calm and safe (S5-6), M5 Work as a team (S7-8).

## Targets (by 15 Jan)

At least 80% of duties done well; can explain their duties (Duties Talk 6+/8); understand what a true leader is; +0.6 confidence; three council-led meetings; students naming the Head Boy or Head Girl 90%+; zero reports of council members touching or punishing students.

## Principles

- Council members remind, reset and report. They never hit, touch, punish or shame anyone, and never step into a fight.
- The late-comers register contains names, so it is private (never displayed or read aloud, two members, open place, no punishment).
- Results are reported honestly: a short programme shows direction, not proof of cause.
