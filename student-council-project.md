# Student Council Leadership Programme

A training programme for the 12-member student council (Std 6-7) of a PMC school. It builds role clarity, calm conflict handling, confidence and follow-through, and is designed so the next council can repeat it without the original facilitator.

## The problem

Late arrivals, class-start delays, frequent fights, students not listening to teachers or the council, low grades, and a council that has no training and no written roles.

## What was built

All files are in [student-council/](student-council/). Each Word file has a PDF print copy.

| File | What it is |
|------|------------|
| [01_HM_Proposal](student-council/01_HM_Proposal.docx) | One-page summary plus the proposal for the Headmaster |
| [02_Action_Plan_and_Timeline](student-council/02_Action_Plan_and_Timeline.docx) | Phases, dates, roles, preparation, risks, meeting checklist |
| [03_Curriculum_and_Facilitator_Guide](student-council/03_Curriculum_and_Facilitator_Guide.docx) | Six 45-minute sessions in the 5 Magic Questions format, with baseline/endline sessions and handover notes |
| [04_Council_Handbook](student-council/04_Council_Handbook.docx) | Charter, code of conduct, Remind-Reset-Report ladder, role cards for all six posts, meeting and initiative templates, duty log, handover checklist |
| [05_Assessment_Pack](student-council/05_Assessment_Pack.docx) | Baseline and endline tools: self-assessment, two parallel quiz forms, scenario task with rubric, teacher rating, classmates' survey, observation checklist, tally sheet |
| [06_ME_Plan](student-council/06_ME_Plan.docx) | Indicators, targets, calendar, analysis, limits, and a resume/showcase impact template |
| [07_Data_Tracker](student-council/07_Data_Tracker.xlsx) | Excel tracker that computes baseline vs endline change and draws charts |

## Timeline (window 25 Nov 2026 to 10 Jan 2027; 25 Dec is the only holiday)

| Mon 4 to Thu 7 Jan | Endline |
| Fri 8 Jan (by Sun 10 Jan) | Report and showcase |
| After 10 Jan | Handover by 15 Jan; light follow-up about four weeks later |

Saturdays are school days and serve as spare slots. The endline and report can move to 11-15 Jan if needed. Any exams or events in the window still need checking against the school calendar.

## Principles

- Council members remind, reset and report. They never physically stop a fight or punish anyone.
- Late and incident counts are kept by teachers, without student names.
- Results are reported honestly: a short programme shows direction, not proof of cause.
